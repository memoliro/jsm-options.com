What is an Option? It's just a reservation ticket.

If you've ever paid to reserve something — sneakers, a PS5, concert ticket, or a house — you already understand options. Let's make it simple for all ages.

Story 1: The $100 Sneakers

You see limited sneakers for $100 at the store. You don't have $100 today, but you have $5. You ask: "Can I pay $5 now to reserve the right to buy them at $100 any time in the next 30 days?"

The store says yes. That $5 is called Premium. The $100 is Strike. The 30 days is Expiry. The sneakers are Underlying.

What happens?
  • If sneakers go to $150 — you still buy at $100. Profit $50 - $5 = $45.
  • If they drop to $70 — you walk away. Loss only $5.

That contract for $5 is a Call Option. CALL = right to BUY.

Story 2: The House — CALL vs PUT

CALL: Pay small fee now to lock right to BUY later at today's price. Good if you think price will GO UP.

PUT: You own a house worth $200k. Pay fee to lock right to SELL at $200k later. If market crashes to $150k, you still sell at $200k. Good if you think price will GO DOWN.

Key: It's a RIGHT, not obligation. You can walk away. Max loss = premium paid.

Story 3: Concert Tickets

Taylor Swift tickets $200 today, concert in 30 days. You pay $20 to reserve right to buy at $200. That's a Call with 30-day expiry.

If resale goes to $400, you buy at $200, sell at $400 = +$180. If resale drops to $100, you let it expire. Loss $20 .

Expiry = date your ticket dies. After expiry, worth $0.

The 5 Words You Must Know

  • Underlying: The real thing — Apple stock, Bitcoin, or those sneakers.
  • Strike: Agreed price — "I can buy at $100 no matter what."
  • Premium: What you pay now — $3, $5. Max loss if trade fails.
  • Expiry / DTE: Deadline — how long reservation lasts.
  • CALL vs PUT: Call = right to BUY (bullish), Put = right to SELL (bearish).

Long = Buy, Short = Sell. "Long Call" = you bought a Call.

How You Make Money — Payoff Picture

Read it: Flat line at -$3 = loss if below $100. At $100 Strike, it rises. At $103 (Strike+Premium) you break even. Above $103 — profit, unlimited.

For All Ages formula: Breakeven = Strike + Premium. $100 + $3 = $103 must be crossed to profit.
