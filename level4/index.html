Cash-secured put (CSP)

Sell a put and keep cash to buy 100 shares at the strike if assigned. Max profit = credit; large downside if stock collapses.

Why longer DTE? Better premium vs risk, time to roll or close before expiry gamma spikes. Prefer liquid strikes.
