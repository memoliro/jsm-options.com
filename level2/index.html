Level 2 • First positions

Your first three options positions.

Use the same core concepts from Level 1, but now see how an actual position behaves at expiration.

1 • Bullish

Long Call

Buy a $100 call for a $3 premium. Maximum expiration loss is the $3 premium per share, while upside is theoretically unlimited.

Breakeven: $103 at expiration.
2 • Bearish

Long Put

Buy a $100 put for a $3 premium. The put can gain value as the stock falls, while the maximum expiration loss is the $3 premium per share.

Breakeven: $97 at expiration.
3 • Defined risk

Bull Call Spread

Buy a $95 call for $6 and sell a $110 call for $2. Net debit is $4 per share, or $400 for one standard contract. The upside is capped by the short call.

Net debit
$400
Breakeven
$99
Max profit
