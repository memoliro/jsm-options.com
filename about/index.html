About JSM Options

jsm-options.com is a free educational site for learning listed equity options — calls, puts, spreads, and multi-leg structures — with plain language and a browser-based Strategy Builder and Backtester.

What you can learn here

  • Basics — what an option is, calls vs puts, strike, premium, expiration, the ×100 multiplier
  • Fundamentals — long call, long put, covered call, intrinsic vs time value, simple Greeks
  • Spreads — vertical spreads and the iron condor (defined risk)
  • Advanced — cash-secured puts, the Wheel, calendars, LEAPS, longer DTE ideas
  • Strategies library — many named structures with outlook and risk notes
  • Builder — build legs, view expiry vs theoretical payoff, and inspect educational Greeks
  • Backtest — test a structure on a simulated path now, or on stored CBOE sessions as that history grows

How the Builder works

The JSM Options Strategy Builder is a free, fully interactive options strategy builder and simulator. It runs in your browser. No sign-up and no account. Create and analyze multi-leg strategies for SPX and equity options, then read the payoff chart, Greeks, profit and loss, break-even, probability of profit, and expected move on one page. Illustrative prices use a Black–Scholes-style model from the spot, IV, rate, and dividend you set. It is ad-free, beginner-friendly, and detailed enough for people who already trade. It is not a live options chain, not a broker, and not a recommendation to trade.

How the Backtest works

The Backtest page also runs in your browser. Until stored CBOE sessions are long enough, the default path is a simulated underlying. Stored sessions are daily delayed snapshots, not a full historical option tape. Results are educational, not a forecast.
