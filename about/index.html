About JSM Options

jsm-options.com teaches listed equity options with plain language and a free, browser-based strategy builder. No account required for the simulator; calculations run on your device.

Curriculum today: Basics (basics), Fundamentals (first trades + Greeks), Spreads (verticals & iron condor), Advanced (calendars, LEAPS, 45+ DTE), plus the interactive Builder.

We are not a broker and do not execute trades. See the Disclaimer and Privacy Policy.
