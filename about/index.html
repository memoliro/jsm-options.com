About JSM Options

jsm-options.com is a free educational site for learning listed equity options — calls, puts, spreads, and multi-leg structures — with plain language and a browser-based Strategy Builder.

What you can learn here

  • Basics — what an option is, calls vs puts, strike, premium, expiration, the ×100 multiplier
  • Fundamentals — long call, long put, covered call, intrinsic vs time value, simple Greeks
  • Spreads — vertical spreads and the iron condor (defined risk)
  • Advanced — cash-secured puts, the Wheel, calendars, LEAPS, longer DTE ideas
  • Strategies library — many named structures with outlook and risk notes
  • Builder — build legs, view expiry vs theoretical payoff, and inspect educational Greeks

How the Builder works

The Builder runs in your browser. Illustrative pricing uses a simple Black–Scholes-style model with the inputs you set (spot, IV, rates, dividends). It is not a live options chain, not a broker, and not a recommendation to trade.

What we are not

  • Not a broker-dealer — we do not open accounts or execute orders
  • Not investment, tax, or legal advice
  • Not a signal service or “get rich quick” program

Options can expire worthless and losses can be substantial. Read the Disclaimer before using any material on this site.
