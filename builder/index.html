Terms Short explanations of terms kept in English

These terms stay in English on purpose; meanings are below.

Call
An option giving the right to buy the Underlying at a Strike.
Put
An option giving the right to sell the Underlying at a Strike.
Strike
The fixed price the option can be exercised at.
Expiry / expiration
The date the contract ends; DTE is days left until then.
DTE
Days to expiration: calendar days left until expiration.
Moneyness
Where Strike sits vs Underlying price: ITM, ATM or OTM.
ITM / In-the-money
Call: Underlying > Strike; Put: Underlying < Strike. Has intrinsic value.
ATM / At-the-money
Strike is very close to Underlying price.
OTM / Out-of-the-money
Call: Underlying < Strike; Put: Underlying > Strike. Intrinsic is zero.
Premium
Option price per share; seen as Bid / Ask / Last.
Bid / Ask / Last
Bid, ask, and last trade price.
Long / Short
Long = buy (rights); Short = sell / write (obligation).
Debit / Credit
Debit: net premium paid. Credit: net premium received.
Intrinsic / extrinsic
Intrinsic: the ITM part. Extrinsic: Premium from time and IV.
Underlying
The stock, index, or asset the option is on.
Greeks
Risk measures: Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega.
Delta
Approx. option-price change when Underlying moves $1.
Gamma
How much Delta changes when Underlying moves.
Theta
Expected time decay after one day.
Vega
Approx. Premium change if IV rises one point.
IV / implied volatility
Market-priced expected swing; inflates or deflates Premium.
Payoff
P/L vs price; usually drawn at expiration.
Break-Even
Underlying price where the trade is flat at expiration.
Assignment
Exercise of a short option; you may deliver or take stock.
Spread
A structure with several legs in the same or nearby expirations.
Vertical
Same expiration, different Strikes.
Calendar / horizontal
Same Strike, different expirations.
Butterfly
Three-strike structure: short in the middle, long on the wings (or the reverse).
Iron Condor
OTM Put Spread + OTM Call Spread; Neutral, defined risk/reward.
Covered Call
Long stock + Short Call; extra income, capped upside.
Cash-Secured Put / CSP
Cash-backed Short Put; you may buy stock if it falls.
Wheel
Cycle: buy stock via CSP, then write a Covered Call.
LEAPS
Long-dated options (often 1+ year to expiration).
Bullish / Bearish / Neutral
Up, down, or sideways view.
Hedge
An offsetting position that reduces current risk.
Liquidity
Tight Bid–Ask and depth; easier to enter and exit.
Roll / Roll-out
Close the open leg and reopen it at a later expiration.
Earnings
Earnings print; IV often rises into the event.
